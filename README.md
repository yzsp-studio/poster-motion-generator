## 🇨🇳 中文

把上传的海报转成 8—15 秒一镜到底的动态海报视频。先强制校验海报已上传，未上传则只提示上传、不进入分析；随后分析比例、主体、海报类型、版式层级、文字与动效潜力，并做方向交叉验证（数值 >1 判横版、<1 判竖版、≈1 判正方形，矛盾时只重分析一次）。接着提供 8 个动态方向供选择，推荐「视觉元素渐进激活成版」与「纸卷展开成版」，用户选定后写 Final_Video_Spec 锁定比例、时长、一镜到底与锁版策略。分镜严格按「背景结构 → 视觉锚点 → 主标题/Logo → 辅助信息/装饰 → 收束 → 微动态」递进，任一时刻只有一个元素或层组主动入场，提示词显式使用动效术语并严格对齐音效时序。上传海报是最终锁版参考而非必须的开场帧，核心内容不裁切不拉伸，可延展背景色调填满画幅，文字保持清晰可读且就位后完全静止。不用于从零设计海报、多镜头剧情片或普通幻灯片。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 上传海报 → 8—15 秒一镜到底动态海报，无剪辑无转场
- 8 个动态方向：渐进激活成版、纸卷展开、裸眼 3D 破框、视差唤醒、粒子重组等
- 比例映射与方向交叉验证，任意比例海报都接受，核心内容不裁切不拉伸
- 五层拆分（背景/核心识别/主标题/辅助信息/装饰）+ 文字可读性保护
- 时间码分镜 + 动效术语库：果冻弹跳、逐字书写、卷轴展开、光束扫掠、粒子重组等
- 音效严格对齐元素入场时序，未入场元素不提前出声，锁版后降为持续低位氛围

## 🇬🇧 English

Converts an uploaded poster into an 8–15 second single-continuous-shot motion poster video. It first hard-gates on the poster actually being uploaded — otherwise it only asks for one and stops — then analyses aspect ratio, subject, poster type, layout hierarchy, text and motion potential, and cross-checks orientation (a ratio above 1 means landscape, below 1 portrait, near 1 square, re-analysing only once if values disagree). It then offers eight motion directions, recommending 'visual elements progressively activate into the final layout' and 'paper scroll unfurls into the layout', and once a direction is chosen writes a Final_Video_Spec locking ratio, duration, the single-shot rule and the lock-up strategy. The storyboard advances strictly through background structure, visual anchor, headline/logo, supporting text and decoration, settle, then micro-motion, with only one element or layer group entering actively at a time, and prompts use explicit motion vocabulary with sound cues tightly aligned. The uploaded poster is the final lock-up reference rather than a required opening frame: core content is never cropped or stretched, background tone is extended to fill the frame, and text stays legible and completely still once placed. Not for designing posters from scratch, multi-shot narrative films or ordinary slide decks.

### ✨ What it does

- Uploaded poster to an 8–15 second single continuous shot with no cuts or transitions
- Eight motion directions: progressive activation, paper-scroll unfurl, naked-eye 3D breakout, parallax wake-up, particle reconstruction and more
- Ratio mapping with orientation cross-validation: any poster ratio is accepted and core content is never cropped or stretched
- Five-layer breakdown (background, core identity, headline, supporting info, decoration) with text-legibility protection
- Timecoded storyboard plus a motion vocabulary: jelly pop, kinetic typography, scroll unroll, light sweep, particle reconstruction and more
- Sound cues aligned to element entry order, no early SFX for unentered elements, and a settled low-level ambience after lock-up
